Disconnect — Scowtt Internal Finance

Effective date: October 5, 2026

To disconnect this private app from QuickBooks Online:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as a company admin.
  2. Open Apps / Integrations (or Settings → Apps).
  3. Find "Scowtt Internal Finance" and disconnect / revoke access.

After disconnect, Scowtt's internal tools will stop receiving QuickBooks data until an admin reconnects.

You can also ask madhuj@scowtt.com to revoke tokens on Scowtt's side.
