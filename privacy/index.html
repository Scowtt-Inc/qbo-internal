Privacy Policy — Scowtt Internal Finance

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This privacy policy covers Scowtt's private QuickBooks Online app, "Scowtt Internal Finance." The app is for Scowtt's own internal finance tools only. It is not offered to the public and is not listed in Intuit's App Store.

What the app accesses

With an authorized Scowtt QuickBooks Online admin's consent, the app may read Scowtt's QuickBooks Online accounting data, such as reports, balances, and transactions, so Scowtt staff and internal tools can do month-end close and related finance work. It may also create journal entries, but only after an authorized Scowtt finance person approves each one. It never deletes QuickBooks records. Every write is logged.

How we use the data

QuickBooks data is used only for Scowtt's internal finance and accounting purposes. We do not sell it. We do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Who can see it

Access is limited to authorized Scowtt personnel and systems that need it for that work.

Storage and security

Connection credentials (such as tokens) are stored securely and used only for the purposes described above.

Disconnect

A QuickBooks Online company admin can disconnect the app at any time from QuickBooks Online Apps / Integrations, or by following the Disconnect page for this app.
